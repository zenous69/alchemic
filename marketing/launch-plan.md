# Launch plan — Kit Autónomo España

Goal: cover ~100 €/month. At ~15 € average that's **~7 sales/month**. Distribution
is the whole game; the product is built. Below is the concrete plan, ordered by
effort-to-payoff.

## 0. Setup (one-time, ~1 hour — needs you)
- [ ] Create a **Gumroad** (or Payhip / Lemon Squeezy) account with your autónomo
      details. Lemon Squeezy / Payhip act as merchant of record and handle EU VAT
      (MOSS) for you — useful selling to other EU freelancers.
- [ ] Connect **Stripe** for payouts.
- [ ] Upload `Kit-Autonomo-Espana.xlsx` + `Guia-Autonomo-Espana.pdf` as the product.
- [ ] Paste the copy from `listing.md`, set price 12 € launch / 19 € normal.
- [ ] Take the 4 screenshots listed in `listing.md` (open the xlsx, capture tabs).
- [ ] Also publish a **Google Sheets version** (File → Import the xlsx) and offer
      "Hacer una copia" link — many autónomos prefer Sheets to Excel.

## 1. Free, warm channels (week 1)
These are communities where Spanish freelancers already gather and ask exactly
the questions this product answers. **Give value first, link second** — answer a
real question, then mention the kit. Do not spam.
- **Reddit:** r/Autonomos, r/spain, r/empleos_es, r/SpainFinance. Write a genuinely
  useful post ("Cómo calculo el 303 y el 130 cada trimestre") and link the kit at
  the end.
- **Forums / FB groups:** "Autónomos España", "Freelance España", Infoautónomos
  community comments, Loentiendo blog comments.
- **Your own network:** if you're an autónomo, you know others. Direct share.

## 2. SEO / content seed (weeks 1–4, compounding)
- [ ] One **simple landing/blog post**: "Modelo 303 y 130 explicados + plantilla
      gratis vs. de pago." Rank for long-tail "plantilla excel autónomo iva irpf".
- [ ] Offer a **free lite version** (Facturas + Gastos only, no auto-303/130) as a
      lead magnet that upsells to the full kit. Lite drives traffic; full converts.
- [ ] List on **template marketplaces** with their own traffic: Etsy (digital
      downloads), Creative Market, Notion/Sheets template directories.

## 3. Paid / scale (only after first organic sales prove conversion)
- [ ] Small Google Ads / Reddit Ads budget on "plantilla autónomo" keywords.
- [ ] Affiliate: offer 30–40% to Spanish finance bloggers/YouTubers.

## Pricing & metrics to watch
| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Conversion (visit → sale) | 1–3% is normal for cold traffic |
| Price | 12 € launch → 19 € → test 24 € with more screenshots/reviews |
| Reviews | First 5 reviews matter most — ask every buyer |
| Refund rate | Keep < 5%; clear screenshots reduce it |
